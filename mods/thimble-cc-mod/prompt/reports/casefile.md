A case file gives each case, such as an agent, a run, a session or an account, a section of its own, so a reader can look one case up or compare cases side by side rather than follow one argument. A short opening section says what the cases are, how many there are and what sets them apart. Then comes one section per case, headed by the case's name and what stands out about it, such as `## Agent 3 gave up on the export after two tries`. Each case section gives, in the same order every time, a two-sentence summary, the steps the case took in the order they happened, one cited bullet each that starts with its time or step, one or two short quotes from the case's own records, and the outcome. Keep each case to about 150 words, so the cases read as comparable entries, and place a figure only where a card shows that case alone.

The panel draws it as a page with a table of contents from its headings, so a reader jumps to a case. A callout (`> [!NOTE] ...`) can set off what a reader must not miss, and a toggle (`<details><summary>More of its records</summary>` ... `</details>`) what they may skip.
