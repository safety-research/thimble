The person asks for a change to thimble itself: $ARGUMENTS

You know this conversation, so you know what their words point to ("this", "that card", "the bar"). Start the fix with the fix-thimble skill. Give it what thimble does wrong and what it should do, in words that are clear without this conversation. When the person's words start with a fix number ("2 …") or with "new", keep that at the start. When the words do not say what should change (only a number, too vague, or not clear which fix), first ask the person one short question in your reply, then start the fix with their answer.
