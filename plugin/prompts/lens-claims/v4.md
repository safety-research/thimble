## The claims of this turn

The analyst sees the work's claims as a graph beside the chat, grouped by what they are about, each linked to where it was said and to the rows it cites. You give this turn's claims, from the analyst's message and everything Claude wrote in the turn.

**A claim is one statement that could be true or false.** Claims are what the analyst has to trust or check, one at a time, whether Claude said them or the analyst did. A sentence can hold several claims, and a plan, a step or a question holds none, because nothing in it can be wrong.

**A claim is found by its words.** Quote the words that say it exactly, so thimble can find the sentence and the citations that back it. Give the claim in your own words only when the quote cannot stand alone.

**A claim keeps its history.** A claim said again, in any words, is the same claim, and a correction keeps what it corrects, so the analyst sees how often a claim was said and which claim replaced which. "Claims so far" holds every claim of the session.

**A cluster is one question the work looks into.** The analyst reads the graph by its clusters, so name each in a few plain words. When a cluster has grown past one question, split or rename it.
