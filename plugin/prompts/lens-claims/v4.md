## The claims of this turn

The analyst sees the work's claims as a graph beside the chat: each claim a dot in its cluster, with where it was said and the rows it cites. You give this turn's claims. Read the analyst's message and everything Claude wrote in the turn, including its words between tool calls.

**A claim is one statement that could be true or false.** The analyst checks the work by its statements, so a count, a share, a cause, a comparison, an "all" or a "most" is a claim, also when the analyst says it. A plan or a step ("I will load the file") is not, because nothing in it can be wrong. Most turns have two to six claims, and a turn that only plans or asks has none.

**The quote places the claim.** Give 4 to 15 words copied exactly from the message that said it, without a citation's link: for "Alice made [41%](r4#user=Alice@share) of the edits", quote "Alice made 41% of the edits". thimble finds the sentence by the quote and takes its citations as the claim's evidence, so a quote that is not word for word loses the claim. The quote is also the claim's name: give "claim" only when the quote leans on "it" or "this" and does not stand alone.

**A claim said again is the same claim, and a correction keeps the old one.** The analyst sees how often a claim was said and which claim replaced which. When this turn says a claim from "Claims so far" again, in any words, give only "same" with its id and the quote. When it corrects, narrows or replaces one, give the new claim with "revises" and the old id; a claim of this turn has no id yet, so revise it as "#N", its place in your list.

**A cluster holds the claims that answer one question.** The analyst reads the graph by its clusters. Give the id of a cluster so far when the claim answers its question, else a new name of two to five plain words, such as "who made the edits". A new subject, such as another person, file or cause, gets a new cluster. When a cluster is listed as grown, split off the claims that answer another question, or rename it when its name no longer says what it holds.
