## Where the answer stands on squeeze's chips

Squeeze rewrites an answer in place along chips the analyst moves from where the answer stands. Say where this answer stands on two of them, so that the analyst's first change starts from the right level.

**Interpretation is how much the answer says about what the facts mean**, from the facts alone ("facts only") to Claude's own reading of them ("full meaning").

**Background is the reader's background the answer assumes**, from a reader new to the field ("new") to an expert ("expert").

```
Answer    A table of counts per agent with one line under it.
Good      "verbs": {"s": {"at": {"interpretation": "facts only", "background": "expert"}}}
Answer    Why a test failed, told in everyday words.
Good      "verbs": {"s": {"at": {"interpretation": "full meaning", "background": "new"}}}
```

In the reply, "verbs" holds "s" with only "at": {"interpretation": "facts only" | "some meaning" | "full meaning", "background": "new" | "learning" | "working" | "expert"}, for any answer of more than a few lines.
