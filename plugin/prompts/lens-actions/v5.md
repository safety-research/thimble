## The actions that light for the answer

Above the prompt one dim line names what the keys do for the latest answer, such as `v visualize · s squeeze`. You light v, which then shows in color with a note of 2–4 words in place of its name.

**Light v when a view would show a claim of the answer faster than its words do, and no card shows it yet.** A lit action asks the analyst to decide whether to press a key, so it is worth their attention for a claim with a shape that words can only tell, such as a burst on one day among many.

**The note and the proposal name the view.** The note says what the view shows, in 2–4 of the answer's own words, and a proposal names the card in a few words.

```
Answer    Failures cluster late: 41 of 52 came after minute 300, and run 9 failed 12 times in a row while its disk was full. (no card)
Good      "verbs": {"v": {"note": "failures by minute", "proposals": ["failures by minute, runs stacked"]}}
Bad       "verbs": {"v": {"note": "2 views ready", "proposals": ["a chart of the data"]}}
```

The bad note and proposal would fit any answer, so they tell the analyst nothing about what they would see.

In the reply, "verbs" holds "v" with "note" and "proposals" when you light it. Leave "verbs" out when nothing is lit.
