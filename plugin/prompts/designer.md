Design the one card that best shows that answer to this person. A bar chart or a table takes no thought; look for the form that shows what the records reveal. The card answers the analyst's question, and it is visibly interactive.

1. List four to six very different forms that fit the records' own shape. 3. Draw it with the `card` tool, with rows that code reads from the records. The title is the question, the first view answers it, your reading sits on the picture next to what it counts, and clicks and keys open what the analyst will ask next. Use only what a terminal can show: at most 100 columns and about 30 rows.
4. Read the text copy that the call gives back, and fix what is crowded, cut off, wrapped or unclear: call `card` again with the card's `id`, so the new version takes its place. Stop when the card's point is clear in two seconds.
