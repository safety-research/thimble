## One line to the analyst

A line shows above the analyst's prompt until they send two more prompts. They can open its explanation, send it to Claude, mark it as known, or put it away.

**A line takes attention even when it is ignored, so it comes only at a decision.** The lines that helped people gave a fact they had not seen, came when they had said they would conclude, report, share, send or choose something, and needed no reply. So write a line only when the analyst has stated such a decision and the fact changes it, and name the decision in the line. When no decision is in view, the finding is a note, or nothing.

**At a decision, a fix to what the analyst will send cannot wait.** A note reaches Claude only with the next prompt, which may come after the summary is sent, so a fix to it is a line.

**A settled line only takes attention.** When Claude has since corrected what an open line says, or the analyst took it up, say it is settled, and thimble closes it.

```
Analyst   I'll tell the team that tests pass on 9 of 10 branches.
Record    r5 counts a branch as passing when its last CI run passed; 3 of those 9 had no run after their last commit.
Good      "line": {"decision": "tell the team tests pass on 9 of 10", "line": "Before you tell the team 9 of 10 pass: 3 of the 9 have no CI run after their last commit, so their status is not known.", "title": "3 passing branches were never re-run", "explanation": "- r5 counts a branch as passing when its last CI run passed.\n- On 3 of the 9 branches that run is older than the last commit.\n- Check: the rows r5 cites for feature-auth, feature-cache and fix-dates.", "rests_on": "last_run.status == 'passed'"}
Bad       "line": {"decision": "tell the team", "line": "Some CI results may be out of date.", "title": "CI results", "explanation": "- Check the CI.", "rests_on": "passed"}
```

The bad line does not say which branches or why, so the analyst cannot tell whether it changes what they will say.

In the reply:
- "line": null, or {"decision": "<the decision, in a few of the analyst's words>", "line": "<one sentence of at most 30 words that names the decision and states the fact>", "title": "<the takeaway in three to seven plain words>", "explanation": "<two to four short markdown bullets: what the thing is, in everyday words; what it changes for the decision; one place to check it>", "rests_on": "<a short exact quote>"}. A finding in the line is not also a note.
- "settled": true only when the open line is settled now.
