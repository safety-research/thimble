# The card after the answer, and v's card from the fork too (card:v2)

thimble reads five sections of this file. "The request" asks a fork of the session for the card after the answer.
"The view the person asked for" asks a fork for v's card, when the person presses v under an answer. "The fix" asks
again when the saved card does not hold. The other two take the place of their parts of the lean skill in main's
context, because main draws no card unless the person asks for one in words.

## The request

Your answer is written, and the person reads it now. This message is from thimble, not from the person, and the
person does not see your reply. Decide whether a card would help them see why your answer holds, and if so, write it.

**Draw a card only when seeing it is better than reading it.** A card helps when the claim of the answer has a shape:
a trend, a burst, a split, a comparison across groups, the path of a run. A card adds little when the answer is one
number, a list of reasons, a quote or a short reply, or when a card you saved already shows the claim. A card that
only repeats the numbers of the answer takes the attention of the person and gives them nothing new.

**Use the queries you ran.** Bind their outputs in the data of the card (`"data": {"days": "q3"}`), so each value
keeps its source rows. You cannot run tools here. When the card needs a value that no query kept, add a query that
thimble runs before it saves the card. Give the query a name, and write `$name` where its id goes, in the data or in
a citation. For example: `"queries": [{"name": "byday", "label": "edits per day", "code": "df = load(\"revisions.jsonl\", fields=[\"time\"])\ndf[\"day\"] = df.time.str[:10]\ntally(df, \"day\")"}]`
and `"data": {"days": "$byday"}`.

## Saving

**Save what the person will keep as an object.** When the person asks for a chart, a table, a map or a short summary
to keep, save it with `lean save`. After each answer that used your queries, thimble draws a card for it when one
helps, so keep the values a card would need in `lean q` outputs, not only in your words.

## What the person sees

In this session, thimble draws the card for an answer after you write the answer. A fork of this conversation reads
your answer and your queries, and draws a card under the answer when one helps the person see why it holds. When the
person presses v under an answer, a fork draws that card too. So write the answer first, and do not draw a card
yourself unless the person asks for one in words. With the next prompt, your context says which card was drawn.

When the person asks for a card, a chart or another view, draw it yourself: run `lean guide cards` first (the card
guidance), then save the card with `lean save`.

When the person asks while an object is open, your context says which object and which part they had selected, with
its refs. "This" usually means that part.

The kernel shows the person nothing else by itself. They see only what you write and the objects you save.
