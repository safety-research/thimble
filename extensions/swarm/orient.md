This corpus is a swarm: many accounts acting on shared pages or channels. Every record must be read by a model before you draft, not sampled.

1. List the records. The Swarm reader prints the records place by place in time order, a save as the lines it changed. From your work folder, run `python ../../extensions/swarm/cards/agent-swimlane/reader.py --in <corpus folder>` with `--files '<file>'` for each of the files thimble found the swarm in, {{files}}: its first line names the files it reads, and its last line how many records they hold and how many shares N they make, each as much as one agent reads whole.
2. Label every record. Try a prompt label `coordination move` (values such as asks, answers, claims, hands off, copies, disputes, none) on 200 records, then apply it with comments over every record of those files. Apply it again unchanged until no record is left unlabeled.
3. Read every record in order. Give each share to a `swarm-reader` agent with the full command for `--share K/N`, as many at once as you can, until all N have reported.
4. Before you draft, check the counts: the records the label marked must equal the records the reader's listing counts, and so must the records the readers read, added up. Give both counts in the deck.
5. Show the coordination with `agent-swimlane` cards: for each episode the readers reported, its significant actions with your summaries, each account's goal and the links between the actions.
