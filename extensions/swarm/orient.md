This corpus is a swarm: many accounts acting on shared pages or channels. Every record must be read by a model before you draft, not sampled.

1. Label every record. Try a prompt label `coordination move` (values such as asks, answers, claims, hands off, copies, disputes, none) on 200 records, then apply it with comments over every record the Swarm view reads. Apply it again unchanged until no record is left unlabeled.
2. Read every place in order. The Swarm reader prints a place's records in time order, a save as the lines it changed: from your work folder, `python ../../extensions/swarm/views/swarm/reader.py --in <corpus folder> --help`. Split the places into shares, busiest first, and give each share to a `swarm-reader` agent with the full command for `--share K/N`, all at once.
3. Before you draft, check the counts: the records the label marked must equal the records the Swarm view counts, and every place must have a reader's report. Give both counts in the deck.
4. Show the coordination with `swarm` cards: for each episode the readers reported, its significant actions with your summaries, each account's goal and the links between the actions.
