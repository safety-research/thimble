thimble is a workbench for an analyst reading a corpus of records, such as agents' transcripts, logs or chat. An extension added to thimble gives the view {{name}}, an overview drawn for one kind of record. You decide whether it fits the corpus the analyst opened, and thimble shows it there when it does. A wrong yes shows the analyst a view that does not fit these records; a wrong no hides a view made for them.
