---
name: Incident timeline
description: What happened in order, grouped into phases, each entry dated, naming who acted and citing the record that shows it.
---

An incident timeline tells what happened in the order it happened, so that a reader can see when each thing occurred, who did it, and what followed from it.

A short opening section says what the incident was, when it began and ended, and who took part. The events follow in time order, grouped into phases. Each phase is one section, headed by what happened in it, for example `## Bob deleted the audit pages within an hour of the vote`. Each event is one bullet. The bullet starts with the event's date and time as the records give them. Then it says who acted and what they did, cited to the record that shows it, for example `- 2024-06-04 14:02 · Bob deleted the page "Audit" [[wiki/log.jsonl#L88]]`. When you inferred a time or an order rather than read it, mark it as inferred. A last short section says what the timeline leaves open. These phases and entries replace the report's length and headings below. The report's markdown and citations still hold.
