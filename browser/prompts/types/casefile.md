---
name: Case file
description: One section per case, such as an agent, a run or a session, each with a summary, what happened in order, quotes from its records and the outcome.
---

A case file lets a reader look one case up, or compare cases side by side, rather than follow one argument. So it gives each case a section of its own, for example each agent, run, session or account.

A short opening section says what the cases are, how many there are, and what sets them apart. The case sections follow it. A case section's heading gives the case's name and what stands out about it, for example `## Agent 3 gave up on the export after two tries`. Each case section gives these parts, in the same order every time:
- a two-sentence summary
- the steps the case took in the order they happened, as one cited bullet each that starts with its time or step
- one or two short quotes from the case's own records
- the outcome

Keep each case to about 150 words, so that the cases read as comparable entries. Place a figure only where a card shows that case alone. This length and these headings replace the report's length and headings below. The report's markdown and citations still hold.
