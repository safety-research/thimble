---
name: Case file
description: One section per case, such as an agent, a run or a session, each with a summary, what happened in order, quotes from its records and the outcome.
---

A case file gives each case, such as an agent, a run, a session or an account, a section of its own, so a reader can look one case up or compare cases side by side rather than follow one argument. A short opening section says what the cases are, how many there are and what sets them apart. Then comes one section per case, headed by the case's name and what stands out about it, such as `## Agent 3 gave up on the export after two tries`. Each case section gives, in the same order every time, a two-sentence summary, the steps the case took in the order they happened, one cited bullet each that starts with its time or step, one or two short quotes from the case's own records, and the outcome. Keep each case to about 150 words, so the cases read as comparable entries, and place a figure only where a card shows that case alone. This length and these headings take the place of the report's below, whose markdown and citations still hold.
