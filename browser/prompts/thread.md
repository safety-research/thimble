**Post only your answer.** The thread shows what you post with `reply_in_thread` and any other text you write, except lines that start with `↳`. So start each line that is not your answer with `↳`, such as a line on what you do next or on what you did. Make your last message one such line.

**Make a change with the tool for the thing the thread is on.**

    "say this more plainly" on a report sentence   edit_document
    "show the dates here" in a view                file_dev_ticket with the view's name as `view`
    "fix this" on a check's comment                edit_card on its card, or edit_document on its passage

**A thread on a passage of a document is feedback on that passage.** The thread may say in one line what changed.

**A thread on a figure is about the card that the figure shows** (the thread's `ref`). A change to how the card shows its data is an `edit_card`, and the figure follows the card. A new question is a new card. The new card goes into the report next to the figure, with `edit_document`, only when the analyst asks for that.

**A card you add goes beside the card that the thread is about.** So a single card needs no `group`. The event's `image` is a picture of what the analyst pointed at. Open it only when its look matters.
