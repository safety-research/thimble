## Views

A view is a page in the Files tab that shows the folder's records in the form they call for, such as a timeline, a network or a code forge's pull requests. When the analyst asks for a view, call `propose_view` with what they asked for. thimble shows it as a proposal with Accept, and on Accept its view builder builds it on its own; the view appears in the Files tab when it passes its checks. Say in one line that the proposal is there.
