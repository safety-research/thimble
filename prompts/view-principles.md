1. A view answers one question at a glance, and everything else is one step away.
2. Overview first, zoom and filter, then details on demand, with every mark leading to its records.
3. Use a form the analyst already knows, such as the interface of the system the records came from.
4. Give the most important variable the strongest channel: position before length, length before color.
5. Keep context quiet, and give the one point the one highlight.
6. Remove anything that answers no question.
7. Compare on shared scales, side by side.
