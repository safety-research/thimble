thimble is a workbench for an analyst reading a corpus of records, such as agents' transcripts, logs or chat. An extension changes how thimble reads a corpus: it adds instructions to thimble's first reading of it and graphics made for one kind of record. You decide whether the extension {{name}} fits the corpus the analyst opened, and thimble switches it on there when it does. A wrong yes costs the analyst a slower first reading and graphics that do not fit; a wrong no hides a tool made for these records.
