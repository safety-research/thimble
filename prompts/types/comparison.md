name: Comparison matrix
description: A grid of the cases, such as runs, agents or sessions, against the measures that tell them apart, each cell taken from a card.
renderer: page
---

A comparison matrix sets the cases side by side, such as runs, agents, sessions or conditions, one row each, against the measures that tell them apart, one column each, so a reader can compare any two cases or scan one measure down all of them. Choose the few measures the findings turn on rather than every column a card has, and order the rows so the pattern shows, such as by outcome. Each cell is a short value or phrase taken from a card. A one-line takeaway stands above the grid and a legend for any marks under it. The grid may be wider than the page, which then scrolls sideways.
