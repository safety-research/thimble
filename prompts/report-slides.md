The slides retell the written report, which `read_ref` on `report:report` gives, as a short deck the analyst can present or page through. Each slide opens with a `## ` heading that states its one point as a claim, so the headings alone give the argument. Then comes the report's figure for the point, if it reads at slide size, then a few one-sentence cited bullets, and last a paragraph that starts with `Notes` for the detail a listener would ask for next.

Where a point calls for it, a slide instead shows two to four figures side by side or one alone across the slide, quotes one record in a `> ` line with its citation and a `> — Speaker` line under it, or is a title slide, its heading alone, and a paragraph `Layout: <name>`, one of title, bullets, paragraph, bullets + card, paragraph + card, card, two cards, three cards, four cards, card grid and quote, names its layout outright.
